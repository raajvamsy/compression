One trick from the Hutter Prize winner: skip tiny updates

The 2024 Hutter Prize entry (fx2-cmix) skips the mixer's learning step when its guess was already almost exactly right. We expected it to be faster. Instead it made files smaller. Our explanation: on near-certain bits, every small update keeps pushing the mixer's weights further and further, and that drift hurts the next uncertain bit. One line of C, same speed.

One core, same settings and lens set, before vs after (threshold 0.0025). Now built in. The full benchmark and lossless proof are re-running.
